Questions

Frequently asked

How do you determine my cash offer?

We look at your property's condition, location, and recent comparable sales in the area, then subtract the cost of any needed repairs. You'll always see a clear, fair number with no hidden math.

Are there any fees or commissions?

None. There are no agent commissions, no closing costs charged to you, and no hidden fees. The offer you accept is the amount you receive at closing.

What if my house needs a lot of repairs?

That's fine — we buy properties in any condition, including major structural issues, fire or water damage, and homes that haven't been updated in decades.

How fast can we close?

As fast as 14 days if needed, or on a timeline that works better for you. There's no pressure either way.

Am I obligated to accept the offer?

Not at all. Submitting your property gets you a no-obligation offer. You're free to accept or decline the offer.
