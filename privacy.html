Privacy Policy

Effective October 5, 2026

GTA Real Estate Ventures LLC ("we," "us," or "our") operates gtatxhomebuyers.com under the name GTA Home Buyers and buys houses directly from homeowners in Texas. This policy explains what information we collect through gtatxhomebuyers.com, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Information we collect

  • Information you give us. When you submit our form, we collect your name, phone number, email address, property address, and any details you choose to share about the property.
  • Text message consent record. If you agree to receive text messages, we keep a record of that choice, including the date and time you submitted the form and the wording you agreed to.
  • Basic technical information. Our website loads fonts from Google Fonts, so Google may receive your IP address and browser details when you visit. This website does not currently use advertising trackers or analytics tools.

2. How we use your information

  • To respond to your request and evaluate your property.
  • To prepare and present a cash offer.
  • To contact you by phone, email, and, if you agreed, text message about your inquiry.
  • To keep business records and comply with the law.
  • To prevent fraud and protect our rights.

3. Text messaging and your mobile information

If you check the text message consent box on our form, we may text you at the number you provided about your property inquiry. Message details, including how to opt out, are in our SMS Terms.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties.

You can stop text messages at any time by replying STOP to any message.

4. How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only in these limited situations:

  • With service providers that help us run our business, such as our form-processing, phone, and text messaging providers. They may use your information only to provide services to us.
  • With title companies, attorneys, or closing agents, only if you choose to move forward with a transaction and only as needed to complete it.
  • When required by law, a court order, or a legal process, or to protect our rights or the safety of others.

5. How long we keep information

We keep your information as long as needed for the purposes above, including legal and record-keeping obligations. We keep text message consent records for as long as the law and carrier rules call for.

6. Security

We use reasonable measures to protect your information. No website or method of transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Your choices

  • Text messages: reply STOP at any time to opt out.
  • Your information: you may ask us to access, correct, or delete the information we hold about you by calling or texting (361) 331-0630 or by submitting our website form and noting your request. We will respond as required by applicable law.

8. Children

Our website is intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date at the top shows when it was last changed.
